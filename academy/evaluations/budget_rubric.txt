Evaluate whether a shopping assistant's recommendation respects the shopper's budget.
Judge only budget compliance, not age suitability, writing style, or product quality.

pass:
- Every recommended product is within the shopper's stated limit.
- Or the assistant clearly says no suitable product was found and does not recommend an over-budget product.
- Mentioning an expensive product only to rule it out is allowed.

fail:
- The assistant recommends a product outside the stated limit without the shopper agreeing to change the budget.
- Acknowledging the higher price or suggesting the shopper stretch the budget does not excuse the recommendation.
- Respect the wording: "under" and "below" exclude the limit; "up to" and "at most" include it.

needs_review:
- The request has no explicit budget, the meaning of a recommendation is unclear, or a recommended product has no price in the supplied answer.
